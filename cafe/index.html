A counter in Bahary

Coffee before the corniche gets loud.

Bahari Beans is a small counter on the Bahary side of Alexandria. Espresso, Turkish coffee, and a few things from the oven. Walk in and collect it on your way along El Gaish Road.

The list

What we actually make

If it is not written here, we are not making it today. Prices are in Egyptian pounds.

Coffee

  • Espresso 35

    A short, dark shot. No flavours.

  • Turkish, plain 25

    Cardamom if you ask.

  • Cortado 50

    Espresso with a little warm milk.

  • Flat white 60

    More milk, still led by the coffee.

  • Spanish latte 65

    Condensed milk, stirred in.

Cold

  • Mint lemonade 40

    Crushed mint, not a syrup.

  • Orange and blossom 45

    Orange juice, a drop of orange blossom.

  • Cold brew 55

    Steeped overnight, poured over ice.

From the oven

  • Basbousa 30

    Semolina cake. Warm before noon, gone after.

  • Spinach fatayer 35

    Sesame on the edge.

  • Halloumi on baladi 75

    Tomato, zaatar, olive oil.
