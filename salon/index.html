Lumière

One chair, booked before you climb the stairs. Cuts, colour, and a blow-dry, in a room in San Stefano. This is a concept for a fictional salon.

The list

Prices in Egyptian pounds. The time is a guide for the visit, not a promise to the minute.

  1. Cut and finish

    450 EGP

    45 min. A wash, and a look at what your hair is already doing.

  2. Restyle

    650 EGP

    75 min. When the length has to change, not just the ends.

  3. Root colour

    750 EGP

    90 min. Regrowth only. Bring a photo of the colour you already wear.

  4. Full colour

    1,200 EGP

    150 min. Roots to ends. A strand test if we have not coloured you before.

  5. Gloss

    550 EGP

    45 min. Shine and tone. It does not cover grey.

  6. Blow-dry

    250 EGP

    40 min. 200 EGP on weekdays before 2pm.

  7. Bridal trial

    900 EGP

    90 min. A separate visit. The wedding day is another booking.

  8. Brow shape

    150 EGP

    20 min. Tweezers. Not a stencil.

  9. Manicure

    200 EGP

    40 min. Clean and short, or a plain polish. No nail art on this list.
